    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: May 14, 2026

    This Privacy Policy describes how Space (the "App", "We", "Us", or "Our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use our mobile application.

    Information We Collect

    When you sign in with Google, we collect:

    • Email address – to authenticate your account and identify you
    • Name – to personalize your experience in the app
    • Profile picture – to display your avatar (optional)

    App Data We Store

    The following data is stored in Firebase Firestore and synced across your devices:

    • Notes and your written content
    • Lists and task completion status
    • Calendar events, dates, times, and descriptions
    • Daily mood entries
    • Mindmap nodes and connections

    How We Use Your Information

    • To provide, maintain, and improve the App
    • To sync your data across devices
    • To authenticate your account via Google Sign-In
    • To respond to support requests

    Data Sharing

    We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We use the following third-party services:

    • Google Firebase – for authentication and data storage. View Firebase Privacy Policy
    • Google Sign-In – for authentication. View Google Privacy Policy

    Data Retention

    Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all data is permanently removed within 30 days. You may request account deletion by contacting us.

    Your Rights

    You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at abhishek.kabi.21@gmail.com.

    Security

    We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
